## The Dragon of Ashfall Peak

Pyrraxes, an ancient red dragon, has claimed the ruined watchtower atop Ashfall
Peak as her lair. She has not been seen hunting in the valley for three seasons,
which the locals take as an ominous sign rather than a relief. Her hoard is
rumored to include a shard of the Sunstone, an artifact the party has been asked
to recover.

## The Tavern at Miller's Crossing

The Sleeping Griffon is the only inn for two days' ride in any direction, run by
a retired mercenary named Oskar Thane. Oskar keeps a ledger of every traveler who
passes through, and will trade information about the road ahead for a fair price
or a good story.

## Mira Duskwood, Warden of the Old Road

Mira Duskwood patrols the stretch of road between Miller's Crossing and Ashfall
Peak. She is quietly investigating a string of disappearances she suspects are
connected to cultists operating out of the collapsed silver mine, not the dragon
the villagers blame.
